We took S9 out of track T5. The question is uncomfortable because the junior work is exactly the work AI now does best. Our table deliverable was a mentoring and skills plan. That is slide three.
02 — What we found

Three clusters, one direction

Learning & skills

  • The craft is no longer learned
  • Learning culture vanishing
  • Senior–junior delta growing

The junior dev pattern

  • Alone with the AI
  • Cannot validate the output
  • “Do they still need to code?”

Cost & dependency

  • Too much AI dependence
  • Tokens burned unsupervised
  • Not production ready
The risk

A missing generation of experts in 5 to 10 years.

Read the three cards as one loop. Juniors learn less, so they work more alone with the AI, so they depend on it more, so they learn even less. The line at the bottom is what I want the room to keep: five to ten years, and nobody left who can debug the outage.
